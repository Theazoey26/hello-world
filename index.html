    KURSEONG


    Originally a remote village,Kurseong has developed by leaps and has made it to the list of best tourist places in West Bengal.


      Best Time To Visite:September-October

    MIRIK


    Mirik is a post card perfect spot in the tranquil hills of Darjeeling.Ramitey Dara is particularly famous for dreamy sunrise and sunset amidst the bird's-eye view of the valley.
